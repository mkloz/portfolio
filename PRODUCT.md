# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and engineering leads assessing Mykhailo Kloz for full-stack web development work. Product collaborators and other developers are secondary visitors. They need to understand his role, inspect real work, judge technical range, and find a direct way to contact him.

## Product purpose

This portfolio presents Mykhailo's work and the engineering decisions behind it. Success means a visitor can identify him as a full-stack developer within the first viewport, find relevant projects quickly, see evidence across interface, API, data, and delivery work, and contact him without searching for details.

## Positioning

The portfolio connects polished interface work to the systems that support it. Project screenshots, repositories, demonstrations, architecture diagrams, and recorded implementation decisions carry the claim.

## Operating context

Visitors usually arrive from a job application, a social profile, or a shared link. They may scan on mobile, compare several candidates quickly, or read a case study in depth on desktop. The main journey is role recognition, project selection, evidence review, then contact.

## Capabilities and constraints

- Preserve React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Vite, routing, theme switching, direct contact behavior, and project data.
- Every project and primary action must remain usable with keyboard and touch input.
- Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and cannot carry essential information by itself.
- Project claims must come from repository data or supplied assets. Do not invent clients, adoption, performance figures, or outcomes.
- Scroll-linked motion is allowed when it explains content, but it is not the default navigation model.

## Brand commitments

Use the name Mykhailo Kloz and the role Full-Stack Web Developer. The voice is direct, personal, and technically specific. The portfolio uses Mykhailo's existing project imagery and an identity-preserving screen-print treatment of his portrait instead of presenting the original photograph as a conventional headshot.

## Evidence on hand

- Personal, education, language, contact, availability, and timeline data in `src/data/me.ts`.
- Six project records with descriptions, technologies, repositories, screenshots, demonstrations, galleries, and build decisions in `src/data/projects/`.
- Generated editorial assets and their provenance in `public/editorial/` and `DESIGN.md`.
- Existing quality checks, accessibility rules, and release scripts in `package.json`, `AGENTS.md`, and `.agents/`.
- No client testimonials, commercial results, or verified audience metrics are supplied. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product principles

- Make the role and strongest work obvious before asking for attention.
- Use project evidence instead of generic capability claims.
- Keep the experience expressive without slowing down scanning or navigation.
- Let visitors choose between a quick overview and deeper technical detail.
- Keep contact details direct and immediately available.

## Accessibility & inclusion

The site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, semantic headings and landmarks, descriptive media alternatives, and mobile target sizes of at least 44 pixels.
